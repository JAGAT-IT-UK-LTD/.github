# Jagat IT UK Ltd

UK-registered software company (Companies House no. 17474995) building focused, pay-per-use web products and mobile apps for the UK and Bangladesh.

## What we build

| Family | What it does |
|---|---|
| **UK Property Checks** (`prop-*`) | Instant, postcode-based reports for home buyers: flood, radon, mining, Article 4, listed status, title and more. |
| **UK Vehicle Checks** (`veh-*`) | Vehicle history, MOT, licence and register checks for cars, bikes, motorhomes, boats, caravans and plant. |
| **UK Exam Practice** (`exam-*`) | Mock tests for SIA, TfL SERU, Driver CPC and Life in the UK, with Bangla explanations. |
| **Jagat Marketplaces** (`jagat-*`) | Bangladesh marketplaces for tutors, drivers, rentals, training and marriage. |
| **Jobs & Sponsorship** (`jobs-*`) | Visa-sponsored job search for the UK and EU. |
| **Business Services** (`biz-*`) | Care staff rota-to-payroll SaaS, telephone answering, company and director checks. |
| **Mobile Apps** (`mobile-*`) | Simple, privacy-first Flutter and Android apps. |
| **Platform** (`core-*`, `design-system`) | Shared payments, email, admin dashboard and design system. |
